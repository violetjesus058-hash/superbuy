# Superbuy Spreadsheet Guide: Marketplace Coverage, QC Research and Fallback Planning

This guide is written for shoppers who want broad marketplace research and a structured fallback plan when comparing purchasing services. It uses sampled community discussions as research signals, not as guarantees or official statements. Always verify the current policy, quote, availability and destination terms on the live service before making a transaction.

## Start with marketplace coverage

Superbuy is often discussed in connection with a broad set of Chinese marketplaces. Use the directory to map a product URL or category to the correct research path, then confirm current link support and item eligibility on the live service.

## Separate tooling from assurance

A clear interface, supported marketplace list or long-running community presence can make research easier, but none of these automatically proves seller quality, item authenticity, shipping reliability or refund success. Record what is documented and what remains an open question.

## QC-photo research

When QC images are available, compare visible features against the listing and note what cannot be assessed from photographs. QC should be treated as an inspection aid with limits, not as a guarantee.

## Fallback planning

If another service becomes difficult to use, compare Superbuy or another alternative using the same fields: item eligibility, service fees, chargeable weight, shipping lines, return terms and communication channels. This makes the decision explainable and reduces reactive switching.

## Spreadsheet scope

The Superbuy Spreadsheet organizes product information and shopping resources. It does not itself provide the live transaction, warehouse or delivery service described in external policies.

## Questions to verify before you proceed

### Does a broad supported-platform list guarantee every item can be processed?

No. Current item, seller, destination and policy restrictions still need to be checked.

### Can QC photos guarantee authenticity?

No. They help assess visible details but do not establish authenticity or future condition.

## Editorial note

This Superbuy page is an informational resource. Community posts can be incomplete, outdated, misattributed or specific to one destination and order. Treat them as prompts for verification, not as proof of platform-wide performance.
